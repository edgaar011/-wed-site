Good to Know

Is there time between the ceremony and the reception?
Yes. The ceremony is at noon and the reception begins at 5:30 PM, so you will have the afternoon to rest before the evening.
Is there parking?
Yes, complimentary parking is available at the venue.
Are children welcome?
Yes! Little ones are welcome. Please include them in your party count when you RSVP.
May I take photos?
We will have a professional photographer, so during the ceremony we ask that you put your phone away and be present with us. At the reception, take all the photos you like.
